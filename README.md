# 5S Fashion — Thời Trang Cho Cả Gia Đình

> **5S Fashion** is a modern Vietnamese apparel brand offering accessible, versatile, and high-quality casual wear designed for daily life and family wardrobes.

---

## 📌 Overview

**5S Fashion** focuses on minimalist, durable, and comfortable clothing that balances modern aesthetics with everyday functionality. Built around core values of comfort, convenience, and affordability, the brand serves a wide demographic with a primary focus on menswear, basics, and matching family collections.

---

## 🏷️ Core Categories

* **Menswear:** Polos, casual shirts, graphic & basic tees, khakis, denim, and lightweight outerwear.
* **Womenswear & Family Sets:** Coordinated casual outfits, loungewear, and seasonal family apparel.
* **Active & Loungewear:** Sweatpants, track jackets, breathable gym wear, and home sets.
* **Accessories:** Underwear, socks, belts, and daily lifestyle essentials.

---

## ✨ Key Highlights

* **Everyday Comfort:** Breathable, stretchable, and easy-care fabrics (Cotton Compact, Coolmax, Spandex blends).
* **Smart-Casual Style:** Versatile designs suitable for the office, casual outings, and home.
* **Accessible Pricing:** High-value product lines optimized for budget-conscious family shopping.
* **Omnichannel Presence:** Nationwide retail storefront network integrated with an active e-commerce platform.
